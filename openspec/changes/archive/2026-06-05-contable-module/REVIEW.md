# Adversarial Review — contable-module

Findings from the multi-agent Review phase. The author already folded Q1–Q9 + F1–F5; the items
below are what the REVIEW found ON TOP of that. Resolve HIGH before `apply`.

## Verdict

APPROVE WITH CHANGES. The design is coherent and faithfully reuses Cliente/Proceso as anchors, reuses ModalidadCobro, keeps the comercial coupling strictly read-only, and the DERIVE-everywhere/snapshot doctrine is sound and matches existing precedents. Money precision, @@map, additive db-push, and the concrete-permiso/módulo-gate model all check out against the live schema and auth middleware. Two blocking items before apply: (1) HIGH — the 5 tenant-scoped leaf tables (Egreso/Nomina/ServicioFijo/CuentaBancaria/CajaMenor) will be ORPHANED on Empresa delete because empresa.delete relies purely on DB Cascade and there is no app sweep; give each leaf an empresaId->Empresa Cascade FK (single path => errno-150-safe) or add an explicit transactional sweep in empresas DELETE, and list empresas.router.ts in Affected Areas. (2) MED — pin three ambiguities that affect financial correctness: the cartera valorPagado matching rule (make configuracionCobroId-vs-(cliente,proceso) mutually exclusive to avoid crediting one Ingreso to multiple carteras), the 'optionally minus Nomina/ServicioFijo' bolsa saldoActual rule (make it deterministic and consistent with totalEgresosMes), and EstadoPagoIngreso.PARCIAL semantics. Also remove CajaMenorMovimiento from the CuentaBancaria delete-block scan (it has no cuentaId field per its own spec), and seed every concrete contable.* clave used by any router or the endpoint hard-500s.

## Conflicts (ordered)

### [HIGH] ORPHANED LEAF ROWS on Empresa delete — real cascade GAP. The 5 tenant-scoped leaf tables (Egreso, Nomina, ServicioFijo, CuentaBancaria, CajaMenor) have NO Cascade FK to anything and only a plain empresaId. empresas.router.ts DELETE /:id (line 194) does a raw prisma.empresa.delete relying PURELY on DB-level Cascade — there is no app-level sweep of denormalized-empresaId leaves. Existing leaf precedents (EtapaProceso/DocumentoProceso) are safe ONLY because their Cascade root Proceso itself has empresaId->Empresa Cascade, so Empresa-delete sweeps them transitively. The contable leaves have NO such transitive path, so deleting an Empresa leaves their financial rows orphaned forever (and CajaMenor orphans => its CajaMenorMovimiento children survive too). Ingreso and Cartera are fine (reached via Cliente->Empresa).

**Fix:** Either (a) give each leaf a real empresaId->Empresa Cascade FK AND verify no second cascade path exists (these tables have no other Cascade FK, so a single empresaId Cascade is errno-150-safe and is actually the cleaner choice), OR (b) add an explicit app-level pre-delete sweep in empresas DELETE that deleteMany's all contable leaf tables by empresaId inside a transaction. Option (a) is strongly preferred and contradicts the proposal's blanket 'leaves have NO Cascade FK'.

### [MED] CajaMenor is declared a 'tenant-scoped leaf (NO inbound Cascade FK)' AND the Cascade root for CajaMenorMovimiento. On Empresa delete, CajaMenor is orphaned (per the gap above); because CajaMenorMovimiento cascades from CajaMenor (which never gets deleted), the movimientos are orphaned too. Same root cause as the leaf-orphan gap but with a second-level amplification.

**Fix:** Covered by giving CajaMenor an empresaId->Empresa Cascade FK (single cascade path; movimientos then sweep transitively Empresa->CajaMenor->CajaMenorMovimiento, exactly the Proceso precedent).

### [MED] requirePermiso does a Permiso.findUnique({where:{clave}}) and throws 500 'Permiso no configurado' if the clave is unseeded (auth.ts:136-138). Therefore EVERY concrete contable.* clave used by a router MUST be present in the CONTABLE seed array BEFORE the router ships, or the endpoint hard-500s. The proposal lists ~18 claves loosely ('reporte.ver/cartera(gestion)/cuenta.*'); the cuenta.* and cartera shorthand must be expanded to concrete claves (cuenta.ver/.crear/.editar, cartera.ver) and each must exist in seed. Wildcards are genuinely unsupported (exact findUnique) — proposal's 'shorthand' caveat is accurate.

**Fix:** Enumerate the full concrete clave list in the CONTABLE seed array (ingreso.ver/.crear/.editar, egreso.*, nomina.*, cajamenor.*, serviciofijo.*, cuenta.ver/.crear/.editar, cartera.ver, reporte.ver — count and cross-check 1:1 against every requirePermiso() call in contable.router.ts). Add a test/grep gate that every requirePermiso clave in contable.router exists in seed PERMISOS.

### [LOW] errno-150 framing is technically a misnomer but the mitigation is materially CORRECT. errno-150 does not fire from 'cascade fan-in' (many tables each with ONE Cascade FK into Cliente is fine — Cliente already has 5 such children: SeguimientoComercial, FaseComercialHistorial, Cotizacion, ContratoComercial, SolicitudAsignacionProceso; adding Ingreso+Cartera = 7 is allowed). The real trap it avoids is a single child having TWO cascade PATHS to one ancestor (e.g. Ingreso with both clienteId->Cliente Cascade AND empresaId->Empresa Cascade => Empresa-delete reaches Ingreso via Empresa->Ingreso direct AND Empresa->Cliente->Ingreso). The proposal's 'empresaId plain no-FK + exactly ONE Cascade root' is the right fix. No action needed on the schema, but the rationale wording in the proposal/specs should be corrected so an implementer does not 'helpfully' re-add an empresaId FK.

**Fix:** Reword the errno-150 rationale: the constraint is multiple cascade PATHS to the same descendant table, not fan-in count. Keep empresaId as a plain indexed column with NO FK on every contable table (as specified).

### [LOW] db push additive claim is sound but one operational risk: adding @@unique([empresaId, tipoServicio, proveedor, periodo]) on ServicioFijo and the @unique on Cartera.contratoId/configuracionCobroId are net-new tables so no existing-data violation. ModalidadCobro reuse (not redefine) is confirmed correct (enum at schema line 757, already shared by ContratoComercial.tipoCobroAcordado + ConfiguracionCobro.modalidadCobro). @@map names do not collide with existing maps (verified against all 26 existing @@map). Money Decimal(14,2)/pct Decimal(5,2) matches existing convention exactly (ContratoComercial.valorAcordado, ConfiguracionCobro.valorFijo).

**Fix:** No conflict; confirm before push that no prior hand-created contable.* table exists in the live DB so db push does not try to alter.

## Cartera / saldo resolution check

Sound in architecture, with two precise risks. SOURCE of valorTotalAcordado: correctly snapshotted from ConfiguracionCobro at cartera-open by modalidadCobro (FIJO->valorFijo; CUOTALITIS/CUOTA_MIXTA->numeroCuotas*valorCuota[+valorFijo upfront]; PRIMA_EXITO->null; fallback ContratoComercial.valorAcordado). All those fields exist (schema 886-906 / 852-865) and contable READS them (never writes) — the read-only boundary is genuinely enforceable since no contable endpoint mutates a comercial table. The snapshot+re-sync (Q4) correctly mirrors the existing cobroSnapshot immutability idiom in SolicitudAsignacionProceso.cobroSnapshot (schema:923), so no drift-by-silent-edit. NO DOUBLE-COUNTING in cartera itself: valorPagado = SUM(Ingreso) only; valorTotalAcordado is a frozen snapshot, not re-summed from cuotas; saldoPendiente = total - paid is a pure subtraction. General income (configuracionCobroId=null) is correctly partitioned OUT of cartera but INTO totalIngresosMes (Q6) — no leak. TWO precision issues: (1) The fallback matching 'configuracionCobroId match OR (clienteId+procesoId match)' can DOUBLE-COUNT an Ingreso if a client has multiple carteras and an Ingreso carries clienteId+procesoId but null configuracionCobroId that matches two carteras' (cliente,proceso) — the OR must be mutually exclusive per-cartera (prefer configuracionCobroId; ONLY fall back to clienteId+procesoId when configuracionCobroId is null AND exactly one cartera matches), else one payment is credited to multiple carteras. (2) PARCIAL semantics (above) must be pinned. Money precision is consistent Decimal(14,2); just ensure sums use Prisma Decimal aggregate (not JS float) end-to-end.

## Multi-tenant / permiso gating

Multi-tenant isolation pattern is correctly specified and matches the live precedent: empresaId always from token via empresaIdRequerido, hard WHERE {empresaId}, assertSameEmpresa on every outgoing scalar FK on CREATE/PATCH (clientes.router.ts:26 is the precedent and only validates the FKs it actually carries — contable's assertSameEmpresa must be EXTENDED to cover the new scalar set: clienteId, procesoId, contratoId, configuracionCobroId, cuentaId, empleadoId, responsableId, registradoPorId, cajaId — none of these exist in the current assertSameEmpresa, so it is net-new code, not reuse). Concrete permiso gating is genuinely wildcard-free: requirePermiso (auth.ts:125) does Permiso.findUnique({where:{clave}}) — exact match, no LIKE/prefix; so contable.* must seed every concrete clave (gap noted). Módulo gate is REAL and correctly described: resolveEntitlements only auto-enables esBaseline modules (entitlements.service.ts:28-34); contable is esBaseline=false (seed:16) and only contracted by independiente_pro/firma/... (seed:84,89) — so a plan without contable gets 403 'Módulo no contratado' (auth.ts:143-144) BEFORE RBAC; esAdminEmpresa bypasses ONLY the RBAC gate (auth.ts:148), NOT the módulo gate — exactly as the proposal claims. soloAdmin restriction for cuenta.*/cartera/reporte.ver maps cleanly onto the existing RBAC loop pattern (seed:63-67). The empleadoId 'must be firm staff, not platform ADMIN (empresaId=null)' rule needs an explicit empresaId !== null AND empresaId === token check in assertSameEmpresa — the existing helper does u.empresaId !== empresaId which already rejects a null-empresaId ADMIN, so the rule is enforceable with the same idiom. One residual hole: nothing prevents a cross-tenant scalar reference that is NEVER validated because assertSameEmpresa only checks the fields the implementer remembered to pass — recommend a single typed assert object covering ALL contable scalar FKs and a unit test that every router CREATE/PATCH routes its body through it.

## Gaps vs the doc

- Empresa-delete orphan sweep for the 5 leaf tables (detailed above) is the biggest unaddressed gap — neither schema nor an app sweep handles it.
- DOC asks for an attachable DOCUMENTS layer (facturas, recibos, comprobantes de egreso, transferencias, cuentas de cobro, comprobantes de nomina, extractos, recibos de servicios, soportes de costas, recibos de caja menor). Proposal reduces this to inline single soporte*Url per row (Q9) and explicitly reserves DocumentoContable without building it. That is a scoped decision, but multi-attachment per row (doc lists multiple distinct doc types) is silently dropped for v1 — acceptable only if confirmed the client accepts one URL per row.
- DOC Nomina lists 'cuentaBancaria?' as free-text; spec offers scalar cuentaId? OR free-text cuentaBancaria String? — ambiguous which ships. Pick one; if both, the report's saldoActual cuenta-linkage (which keys on cuentaId) silently ignores free-text-only nominas.
- No empresa.delete integration mentioned in Affected Areas — empresas.router.ts is not listed as Modified, so the orphan gap will be missed at implementation time.
- CuentaBancaria delete-block (app-level Restrict) must scan FIVE tables (Ingreso/Egreso/Nomina/ServicioFijo/CajaMenorMovimiento) for cuentaId references; spec states this but it is an O(5-query) check easy to under-implement (e.g. forgetting CajaMenorMovimiento, which does not even carry cuentaId per its spec — CajaMenorMovimiento has medioSalida but NO cuentaId field, so listing it in the delete-block scan is a spec inconsistency).
- saldoActual derivation for CuentaBancaria is specified as 'optionally minus PAGADO Nomina/ServicioFijo linked by cuentaId' — the word 'optionally' makes the bolsa balance non-deterministic. Two readers will implement two different saldoActual. Must be pinned: either Nomina/ServicioFijo cuentaId DO or DO NOT debit the bolsa, consistently with totalEgresosMes.
- EstadoPagoIngreso.PARCIAL is summed into valorPagado via valorRecibido. If PARCIAL is meant as a row-level label 'this payment is itself a partial installment', summing valorRecibido is correct; if PARCIAL ever means 'amount field is provisional/unsettled', it overcounts. Define PARCIAL semantics explicitly so cartera valorPagado is sound.
- Reportes period filtering: tables use a String periodo 'YYYY-MM' (Nomina, ServicioFijo) but Ingreso/Egreso/CajaMenorMovimiento use a DateTime fecha*. The report must filter Ingreso/Egreso by a date RANGE derived from periodo while Nomina/ServicioFijo filter by string equality — a mismatch easy to get wrong (timezone boundary on fechaIngreso vs string month). Pin the date-range derivation (inclusive start, exclusive next-month start, server TZ).

## Open questions (recommendations already folded by the author)

- Q1 — Cartera estadoCartera persistence: store estadoCartera as a maintained label (recomputed on Ingreso write, indexable for vencido alerts) while valorPagado/saldoPendiente stay derived, OR derive estadoCartera too (fully pure, no maintained column, alert recomputes at read)? Recommend: store estadoCartera only (derived money, indexable label) — it is the one concession that keeps a vencido-alert index without reintroducing money drift.
- Q2 — Nómina staff ref target now: confirm empleadoId points at Usuario.id today (scalar, no FK) with required nombreEmpleado snapshot, and that the future contratos/Empleado entity will take over by id later. Confirm no light contable Empleado table is created now. Recommend: scalar empleadoId + snapshot, no Empleado entity.
- Q3 — Cartera grain: ONE row per ConfiguracionCobro/contrato (@unique contratoId + @unique configuracionCobroId), with optional procesoId on the row? Or one row per (cliente, proceso)? Recommend: per-contrato head (matches the 1:1 ConfiguracionCobro↔ContratoComercial spine and the cobro-plan-centric loop); proceso is an attribute, not the grain.
- Q4 — valorTotalAcordado: frozen SNAPSHOT at cartera-open (mirrors cobroSnapshot immutability) vs re-sync from ConfiguracionCobro on read? Recommend: snapshot + an explicit re-sync action, so later plan edits don't silently move the agreed total.
- Q5 — Expense aggregation doctrine: confirm the 4 source tables (Egreso por_proceso/general, Nomina, CajaMenorMovimiento, ServicioFijo) are INDEPENDENT and summed SEPARATELY in the report, with NO settling-Egreso emission and Egreso limited to costs not covered by the typed tables. Recommend: yes — one documented helper used by both report and saldoActual; no settling rows.
- Q6 — Ingreso↔plan linkage strictness: must contable Ingreso always carry configuracionCobroId/contratoId for contract income (so cartera valorPagado is exact), or fall back to clienteId+procesoId matching? And confirm general/non-contract Ingreso (configuracionCobroId null) is INCLUDED in totalIngresosMes but EXCLUDED from cartera saldoPendiente math. Recommend: prefer explicit configuracionCobroId, fall back to clienteId(+procesoId); partition general income out of cartera.
- Q7 — radicado snapshot vs procesoId for totalGastosProceso: group by procesoId (stable) and treat radicado as a display/secondary key, with backfill of radicado when the Proceso is later filed? Recommend: group by procesoId; radicado is report-only convenience.
- Q8 — Caja menor multiplicity: header + movimientos (multiple funds per empresa, recommended, matches design) vs a single flat movement table with optional cajaMenorId? Recommend: CajaMenor header + CajaMenorMovimiento (cajaId Cascade) — cleanest derived saldo and the only satellite-of-satellite.
- Q9 — Are cuentaId / soporte fields enough for v1, or is the polymorphic DocumentoContable table needed now? Recommend: inline soporte*Url for v1; reserve DocumentoContable name, add only if multi-attachment per row is required.
